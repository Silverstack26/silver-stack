Help & FAQ

Is TrackMySilver free?

Yes. No ads, subscriptions or paid memberships.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode stores your collection in this browser. Sign in for cloud inventory and sales.

How do I add silver?

Select Add silver, choose a preset or custom item, and enter quantity, fine silver per item and optional purchase details.

What is a troy ounce?

One troy ounce equals approximately 31.1035 grams.

How is my collection valued?

Fine silver weight multiplied by the latest available spot quote. Dealer premiums and collectible values are excluded.

Can I track constitutional silver?

Yes. Presets include 90% dimes, quarters and halves, Morgan and Peace dollars, 40% Kennedy halves and war nickels. Actual circulated weights may be lower.

Can I add custom items?

Yes. Choose Custom / not listed and enter the fine silver content.

How are returns calculated?

Unrealized returns compare spot value with recorded positive purchase costs. Realized returns subtract allocated purchase cost and fees from sale proceeds. Unknown costs are excluded.

What does the growth chart show?

It reconstructs acquisitions from purchase dates of current holdings, not historical account snapshots.

Can I record sales?

Yes, in guest mode and signed-in accounts. Sales deduct inventory and cannot currently be undone.

Can I change my goal?

Yes, in Settings & account or via Edit goal on the overview.

Can I back up my collection?

Download a JSON backup in Settings & account. Import is guest-only and does not restore sales history. Do not import a backup containing sales as a complete restore.

What happens if I clear browser data?

Guest data may be lost. Signing in or out can also clear guest data, so export first. Cloud records are stored separately.

How do I reset my password?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in form and follow the email instructions.

How do I change appearance?

Use the sun/moon button or choose Light, Dark or System default in settings.

Can I use my phone?

Yes, the layout adapts to smaller screens.

How do I report a problem?

Open Feedback in the footer. Include steps to reproduce the issue, but never passwords or sensitive collection details.

Help us improve

Send a bug report, feature request or general feedback. Reports are reviewed as time permits; individual replies are not guaranteed.

Please do not include passwords, financial account details, inventory backups or physical storage locations. Your report is sent to Netlify for review by the site administrator.

Privacy policy

TrackMySilver is an independently operated silver recordkeeping service.

Information and storage

Guest inventory, sales, daily portfolio snapshots and goals are stored in your browser. Appearance preferences and authentication session information may also be stored locally. Registered users provide an email and password through Supabase authentication; inventory, purchase details, sales, daily portfolio snapshots and goals are stored in Supabase.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the site and processes feedback submissions, including optional email and message details. Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage. Gold API supplies silver quotes; jsDelivr delivers the Supabase JavaScript library. These providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details under their own policies.

Use and sharing

Information is used to operate the tracker, synchronize records and respond to feedback. TrackMySilver does not display paid advertising or sell personal information. Service providers process information to deliver their services.

Data management and retention

You can edit or delete inventory entries and export backups. Guest records may be removed by clearing browser data. Cloud records and feedback are retained as needed to operate the service and handle requests; provider backups or logs may persist. Use the feedback form for access, correction or account deletion requests. Do not submit passwords.

Security and changes

No online service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may change as the service evolves. Contact the administrator through Feedback for privacy questions.

Financial disclaimer

TrackMySilver provides estimates and recordkeeping, not financial, investment or tax advice.
